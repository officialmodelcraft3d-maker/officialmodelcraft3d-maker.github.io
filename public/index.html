FAQ

Questions clients ask before they hire us

How long does a SolidWorks model take?
Most parts and small assemblies are delivered in 24 to 48 hours. Complex assemblies, drawings packages and sculptures are quoted with their own timeline, usually within one to two weeks.
How much does 3D CAD modelling cost?
Simple parts start around USD 60 and most assemblies land between USD 250 and 1,500 depending on part count, drawings and revisions. Every quote is fixed-price.
Do you work with clients in the USA, UK, Canada and Australia?
Yes. Most clients are in those four countries. We keep overlapping working hours with each region, invoice in USD, GBP, CAD or AUD, and deliver drawings in the standard your local shop expects.
What files will I receive?
Native SolidWorks files (SLDPRT, SLDASM, SLDDRW), a neutral STEP or IGES, and STL or 3MF for printing. Renders ship as 4K PNG and MP4.
Can you reverse-engineer a part from photos?
Usually. Send photos with a ruler or one known dimension in frame, or a 3D scan. We rebuild the part parametrically so it stays editable and flag any dimension we had to estimate.
Will my design stay confidential?
Yes. We sign your NDA before any files are exchanged and only show a project publicly with written permission.
