FAQ

Reverse engineering questions

Can you reverse engineer a part from photos only?
Usually yes. Photograph the part from several angles with a ruler or calipers visible, and note any critical dimensions. We rebuild it parametrically and list the dimensions we estimated.
What scan formats do you accept?
STL, OBJ, PLY and point-cloud exports from any handheld or desktop scanner. We also work from CMM or caliper measurements.
Will the file work in my CAD software?
Yes. You receive a neutral STEP file that opens in SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Inventor, Onshape, NX and Creo, plus a native file on request.
